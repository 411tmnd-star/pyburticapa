    How to run the crit she described

    1. Settle the standard first. The partner's question was whether AI work gets a different bar. The answer that ended the debate: users judge the output, not the method, so one bar for everything.
    2. Write one OVERALL line. What still has to change and why, in one or two sentences (on her slide: refine the parallelogram, match how a poured drink looks, tune bubble size and mix).
    3. Pin every fix to a place. A letter on the image and the note beside it. Notes are specific and physical: "edges too sharp and glass-like", "bubbles a bit too large", "this area feels heavy".
    4. Refer back instead of repeating. Her sheet says "Same note as A". It keeps 17 notes short.
    5. Go one level deeper than the customer will look. Her phrase. Not perfection, but past what a viewer would consciously notice.
