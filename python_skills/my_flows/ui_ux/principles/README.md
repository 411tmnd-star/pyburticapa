The rules every original page follows. The full texts are the chat skills, copied in [../../../llm_instructions/chat_skills/](../../../llm_instructions/chat_skills/):

## De-zombie a landing page

1. Count the template formula across the pages you are copying from (tinted two-line hero, muted subhead, pill pair, decorative glow, product mock, serif display, KPI numbers).
2. Write a three-line point of view for the business ("charcoal or nothing; you cook, we coach; the table is the product").
3. Rebuild from it with one fact, unit or image only that business has. Every visual choice traces back to the three lines.
4. Show both versions side by side. Worked example: [../rebuilds/ui-specifics-01/dezombie/](../rebuilds/ui-specifics-01/dezombie/).

## Keep an AI-built storefront from looking generic

- Write a one-page brand and customer brief first; give it to the AI every time.
- Save standards as files the AI reads (colours, tone, example pages, things to avoid).
- Give specific prompts with your own beliefs and examples.
- Treat the first draft as unfinished: spacing, small details, empty and error states.
- Click through the whole customer journey like a buyer.
- Ask a second session to critique it; then you decide.
- One person is the editor who can say no after the build.
